                                                                                                                tive and air density and oxygen availability are
                                                                                                                almost half of those at sea level (17), suggesting
                                                                                                                that this tropical bird encounters extreme con-

                                                                                                                cloud fields are considered to be randomly dis-

                                                                                                                these conditions, climbing higher than 1000 m
