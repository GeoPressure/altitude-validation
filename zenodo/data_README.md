# Data for “How accurate is altitude from pressure geolocators?”

Unzip this archive at the repository root, then run `Rscript run_all.R`. The frozen selections supply accepted rolling-window observations directly; raw NOAA annual downloads are not needed to rebuild the numerical analysis. Times are UTC and heights are in metres.

| Path | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `interim/ghcnh/selection-2026-10-13/` | Candidate inventory, fixed settings and order, decisions, accepted hourly pressure and primary ERA5 inputs; cached assessed candidates support the selection comparison | NOAA GHCNh and ECMWF ERA5 |
| `interim/igra-selection-2026-10-13/` | Candidate inventory, settings, decisions, prepared profiles and accepted surface–airborne pairs with primary ERA5 inputs | NOAA IGRA2 and ECMWF ERA5 |
| `interim/analysis-2026-10-13/era5/` | Additional ERA5-Land surface pressure and temperature at the retained observation hours | Copernicus Climate Change Service, CC BY 4.0 |
| `interim/analysis-2026-10-13/historical_ground.parquet` | Fixed five-station historical comparison | NOAA GHCNh and ECMWF ERA5 |
| `interim/indep/`, `interim/gnss/`, `raw/gnss_logs/` | GNSS barometer records, site logs and atmospheric inputs for the 2022–2024 comparison | IGS/EUREF, BKG and ECMWF |
| `interim/meteoswiss/`, `raw/ogd-smn_meta_stations.csv` | SwissMetNet station pressures and barometer-height metadata, 2022–2024 | MeteoSwiss, CC BY 4.0 |
| `raw/era5_invariant.nc` | ERA5 sub-grid orography and land-sea mask | Copernicus Climate Change Service, CC BY 4.0 |
| `raw/ghcnh-station-list.txt` | GHCNh station metadata and WMO identifiers | NOAA NCEI |
| `raw/gpt3/`, `raw/us_nga_egm*.tif` | GPT3 climatological lapse-rate grids and EGM96/EGM2008 geoid grids | Vienna Mapping Functions service and PROJ grid distribution |
| `interim/igra/` | Soundings retained for the separately documented fixed position-displacement experiment | NOAA IGRA2 |

The primary selections cover 2022–2026. Pressure is in hPa for GHCNh, GNSS and MeteoSwiss, and Pa for ERA5 and IGRA2. Selection ZIP downloads and raw annual archives are omitted after validated extraction. Derived error series are regenerated by step 09. Bird tracks are available separately in the Master GeoLocator Data Package, version 2026.0 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.18187093); committed aggregate weights suffice for the analysis. Dataset citations and licences are given in the manuscript and its supplement.
