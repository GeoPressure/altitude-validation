# Online review of the ten largest remaining ERA5 station biases

Author-facing review, 22 October 2026. Stations are the ten largest absolute mean biases for the ERA5 standard-lapse conversion after the terrain and pressure-continuity selection. Numerical results come from `output/drafts/atmospheric-figures/high_bias_terrain_filtered_stations_review.csv`. The seven documented-conflict stations below are excluded from the ground histogram selection through `docs/methods/ghcnh_curation_exclusions.csv`; station metadata are not corrected. Qairoonhairiti and Sayak were subsequently excluded through manual time-series curation, as recorded in `ground-curation-and-spikes-2026-10-22.md`; Issyk remains eligible.

## Interpretation and proposed curation

There is strong independent evidence for metadata conflicts at Kastamonu, Naze, Karabük, Romblon and Campos do Jordão. Khoy and Kokand have additional institutional catalogue evidence supporting site conflation or incorrect reference metadata. Qairoonhairiti, Sayak and Issyk remain unresolved. Stable bias with small temporal SD is compatible with a fixed height or pressure offset, but cannot identify its cause by itself.

The published heights for Kastamonu, Karabük, Romblon, Khoy and Campos do Jordão lie close to the pressure-derived heights. This numerical agreement supports the metadata interpretation; it does not verify an exact barometer elevation or justify correcting height from ERA5. Any correction involving coordinates requires re-extracting ERA5 at the independently established site.

Recommended rule: exclude station records when independent station documentation shows an unresolved conflict in location, elevation or site identity with the archive metadata. Apply the rule in candidate selection so another eligible station can represent the cell. These are documented metadata exclusions, rather than unexplained removal of the largest residuals. Do not automatically exclude the unresolved cases solely because they disagree with ERA5: they could include genuine conversion or representativeness error. If further manual exclusions are chosen, describe their actual basis and their use of the evaluated residual, rather than presenting them as independent quality screening.
