# T10 — Small paired location-displacement experiment

The author requested a compact appendix showing accuracy and precision from 0 to 200 km. This pilot answers the atmospheric-annotation question with fixed observed pressure, time and reference height. It does not test a location-dependent uncertainty lookup or terrain clearance.

## Design fixed before inspecting outcomes

- Ten IGRA2 stations: one randomly selected station in each of five climate classes × two sub-grid terrain-roughness strata (<100 or ≥100 m SD). Seed 20261018, sorted candidates, at least two eligible launches in each quarter of 2024. Prefer Southern Hemisphere candidates in the lower-roughness stratum and Northern Hemisphere candidates in the higher-roughness stratum when available. The resulting design contains six northern and four southern stations; hemisphere and roughness are partly confounded, so this is not a factorial test of their separate effects.
- Eight distinct soundings per station: the two closest to the midpoint of each calendar quarter. The closest retained observed level to 2 km must be within 1.7–2.3 km and have a matched retained surface record. Day/night status is retained but not forced: polar seasons cannot supply both solar day and night. The original reference-QC selection is reused.
- Nominal offsets of 0, 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 km on a sphere of radius 6371.0088 km. Positive distances use eight bearings spaced by 45°. An offset is a deterministic sensitivity scenario, not a sample from the bird's position posterior. The 0 km case is included once per sounding.
- Query surface pressure, 2 m temperature and dewpoint together at the nearest ERA5 grid cell/hour, and use matching ERA5 orography. GPT3 is evaluated at the displaced geographic coordinate and the original UTC date. Preserve each level's original observation time, including any difference between level and surface times. Grid snapping can leave small displacements in the same cell; nominal distances therefore need not produce smooth changes.
- Compare fixed standard atmosphere, ERA5 standard lapse and ERA5 virtual temperature/GPT3. The fixed standard atmosphere is a location-independent control. For absolute altitude subtract the unchanged reported sonde geopotential height; for surface-referenced height subtract the displaced surface error from the displaced level error, as in the main analysis. This uses a surface anchor at the same horizontal displacement; it does not reconstruct an actual uncertain flight path or its endpoint anchors.

All station/launch/location selections are exported before weather retrieval. They are in `stations.csv`, `soundings.csv`, `locations.csv` and `query-plan.csv` beside this note. The pilot has 410 nominal locations, 2,950 unique snapped grid/hour combinations and approximately 924 variable chunks (308 physical chunks × 3 fields) under the current ARCO layout. The cost is mainly HTTP retrieval and chunk decompression, not altitude conversion. Extracted values are cached per station under `data/interim/location_sensitivity/`; successful stations are reused on rerun.

## Outputs and interpretation

The appendix figure plots mean absolute error (MAE) and population SD of error against nominal distance, separately for absolute altitude near 2 km and surface-referenced height. Faint curves show station-level summaries, stronger curves pool the balanced pilot. Each station has eight launches and each positive distance has eight directions per launch, so simple means give equal weight to stations, launches and bearings at each distance. Between-station offsets contribute to the pooled SD; the pooled curve is not the mean of the station SDs. Directional observations are correlated repeated measurements, not independent replicate birds.

Also retain MAE, RMSE, MAE increment relative to 0 km, mean absolute paired altitude change and its 95th percentile in `results.csv`, plus station, direction and roughness summaries. Signed directional cancellation must not be interpreted as negligible annotation error. No bird-flight height weighting is applied because this pilot evaluates a fixed layer near 2 km. Its errors should not replace the main manuscript's flight-time-weighted results.

Ten stations and eight launches each supply an economical sensitivity illustration, not a globally representative validation or a search for rare weather extremes. Coastlines, fronts, pressure gradients and mountainous extrapolation may produce larger effects than the mean. Do not turn this into a universal acceptable geolocation radius. The displacement of ERA5 orography belongs to the atmospheric altitude calculation here; a DEM-based subtraction to estimate height above local terrain remains a separate problem.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run `Rscript run_all.R position figures` (scripts `scripts/analysis/position_sensitivity.R` and `scripts/figures/supp_position.R`; results in `output/position-sensitivity/`). The experiment needs the existing reference data, local GeoPressureR checkout and network access on the first run. The figure script needs only the saved aggregate CSVs and shared plotting palette. `provenance.json` records the actual runtime and exact-location/control checks after completion.
