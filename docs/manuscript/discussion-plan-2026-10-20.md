# Discussion: consolidated argument and evidence

Author-facing review notes, 20 October 2026. The manuscript draft is in `paper/paper.qmd`; this file keeps the agreed argument, evidence boundaries and revision decisions outside publication text.

## Storyline

Better atmospheric conversion is useful for ecological interpretation and practical to adopt. Present ERA5, virtual temperature and GPT3 as one accessible recommendation with complementary contributions: ERA5 delivers the largest improvement over a fixed standard atmosphere; humidity and the climatological lapse rate further reduce systematic flight-height bias. Lead with the finding, bring ecology forward, then explain accessibility, input choices, remaining measurement uncertainty and validation scope. Avoid repeating the introduction's biological motivation or the methods' atmospheric derivation.

## Consolidated paragraph map

1. **Main finding and practical opportunity.** Establish the combined recommendation; use the ground improvement and bird-weighted comparison as numerical anchors; distinguish bias reduction from remaining scatter; introduce open availability of weather and climatology inputs.
2. **Ecological interpretation.** Explain apparent gradual ground-elevation changes caused by weather; connect correction to timing and magnitude of real movements. Discuss low-height turbine exposure, urban/light responses and topography as applications where vertical error can matter. Contrast these with broad descriptions of kilometre-high migration. Recognize seasonal/day-night atmospheric differences; interpret bird weighting as sample-specific and retain height-specific summaries. Cite a concrete light-response example without implying barometric validation or universal ecological sensitivity.
3. **Accessibility of the combined approach.** Recognize station and reanalysis correction precedents alongside continuing standard-atmosphere use. Explain ERA5 coverage, reusable GPT3 climatology and surface humidity availability together. Place GeoPressureR here as the implementation of all components. Use the position-displacement pilot to support correction from approximate positions, distinguishing pooled error changes from individual paired changes and atmospheric annotation from terrain selection.
4. **Input choices and further improvement.** Include the negative ERA5-Land finding and contrast ground offsets with surface-referenced flight errors. Explain why a climatology reduces recurring bias without eliminating profile variability. Acknowledge pressure-level-derived lapse rates in ecology and fuller atmospheric profiles in aviation; frame the additional benefit/data tradeoff as a question requiring comparison, not an established speed or accuracy ranking.
5. **Interpretation and calibration.** Distinguish persistent ground offset, temporal scatter and individual flight error; relate persistent-offset cancellation to comparisons at one site without treating SD as the error of a difference. Retain terrain dependence, sensor offsets/drift and known-height calibration. Position device and grounded-bird studies as complementary evidence. Explain that sounding-surface height is not local terrain clearance; retain a low-height tail example to prevent use of the atmospheric component as total uncertainty. Keep the evaluated-error table here.
6. **Scope of validation.** Cover reference-height metadata, ERA5-dependent screening and its sensitivity result, complementary ground references, assimilation dependence, radiosonde height derivation, ignored drift and land-based/uneven coverage. End on the supported practical implication rather than a catalogue of caveats.
